# PortMem

PortMem checks AI-written drafts against the source documents they rely on. It flags wrong numbers, statements the
sources do not confirm and material provisions a draft leaves out, each with the source passage that decides it.

This plugin gives your assistant two things:

- **The PortMem connector**, which reaches the documents in your PortMem workspace. You sign in to PortMem once
  when you connect it.
- **A skill, `check-against-sources`**, which is PortMem's own method for the job: send the whole draft, settle
  which documents it is checked against, show the review, report what PortMem found without adding to it, and
  check a revised draft against the earlier findings.

## What you can ask

- "Check this draft against my documents."
- "What does the credit agreement say about events of default?"
- "Show my last review. Which findings are still open?"
- "Who signed this report off, and when?"

## What stays with you

Accepting a flagged claim, publishing a record and changing who can see a document are your decisions, made in the
PortMem console. The assistant gives you the link.

## Requirements

A PortMem account. Your documents stay in your own workspace and are visible only to the people you share them
with.
